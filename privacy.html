Privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026.

The Good Part uses a private desktop tool, named "clipping", to upload videos to its own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. The tool is not offered to the public.

Data we use

The tool only requests permission to upload videos to the authorizing account's YouTube channel. It does not collect, sell or share personal data from viewers or other users.

Storage

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer. You can revoke access at any time in your Google account permissions.

Google services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions: open an issue in this project's repository.
